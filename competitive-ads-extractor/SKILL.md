# Competitive Ads Extractor Skill

This tool helps marketers analyze competitor advertising by extracting ads from platforms like Facebook and LinkedIn, then examining successful messaging patterns and creative approaches.

## Core Capabilities

The skill performs several functions: it gathers ads from ad libraries, captures visual copies, analyzes the messaging around problems and value propositions, groups ads thematically, identifies recurring successful patterns, and offers strategic insights about why certain approaches resonate.

## Key Use Cases

Users typically turn to this skill when researching competitor strategies, seeking creative inspiration, understanding market positioning, identifying effective messaging patterns, discovering pain points their audience cares about, and planning campaigns based on proven concepts.

## Practical Application

For instance, extracting Notion's ads reveals they emphasize distinct problems—scattered information across tools, excessive meetings, and lost documentation. Their creative often uses before/after comparisons showing chaotic versus organized workflows. Headlines tend toward benefit-focused language, body copy stays concise, and targeting varies by audience segment (founders, team leads, enterprise, students).

## Important Guidelines

The documentation emphasizes ethical research: using findings for inspiration rather than direct copying, respecting intellectual property, and leveraging insights to create original content. It recommends regular monitoring, testing inspired concepts, maintaining a reference library, and experimenting across multiple platforms.

## Output Options

Results can be delivered as screenshots, analytical reports in markdown format, spreadsheets with ad details, visual presentations, or categorized pattern libraries for future reference.
