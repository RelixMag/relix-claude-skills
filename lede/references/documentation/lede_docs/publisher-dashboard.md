The Customer Data tab in the Publisher App is where you manage your subscriber base and revenue-related records. It connects directly to the Lede Customer Data Platform (CDP) and syncs with Stripe for financial operations.

The sidebar on the left lists the available object types. Click any item to view a filterable, sortable list. Click a row to open the detail view where you can view or edit the record.

## What is the Publisher App?

**URL:** https://docs.joinlede.com/publisher-dashboard/what-is-the-publisher-dashboard/

**Contents:**
- What is the Publisher App?
- Overview
- Accessing the Publisher App
- How the Publisher App is Organized
- Customer Data vs. Settings
- Related Articles

The Publisher App is Lede’s management interface for your publication, at app.joinlede.com. It gives you direct control over your subscribers, your site’s configuration, your analytics, and your support requests.

Using the app, you can manage your subscriber base, configure how your site looks and behaves, set up integrations with third-party services, and control features like paywalls and registration walls — all from a single interface.

The Publisher App is available to your site’s administrators. Sign in at app.joinlede.com with your Lede account, or jump straight there from WordPress: the Lede menu in your WordPress admin sidebar links to Dashboard, Customer Data, Analytics, Support, and Settings, and signs you in automatically — you will not be asked for a password again.

The app has five top-level tabs — Overview, Customer Data, Analytics, Support, and Settings. Two of them do most of the day-to-day work:

Each tab has a sidebar on the left that lets you navigate between sections. The sidebar changes depending on which top-level tab you’re on.
